HexScrawl

HexScrawl is a hex-map drafting program for running and playing hex crawl TTRPG campaigns and Westmarches-style games.

This application is currently in closed alpha, and is not yet released to the public.

Features

Self-Hosted

Your data stays on your machine.

Full Markdown Support

Embed notes notes on regions, hexes, and point-of-interest markers.

External Links

Link directly from the map to a wiki or your Obsidian vault.

Smart Ruler

A configurable ruler tool to estimate travel distance based on terrain.

High-Resolution Exports

Export high-resolution images of your map.

Collaborate

Share editing and viewing links with your friends (may require configuring port forwarding).

Add-Ons

A simple API for adding custom terrain types and tilesheets.

Support

A solo developer doing her best. <3
